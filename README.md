# Sahu Fabrication Works

**Strength in every detail. Built for your space.**

Sahu Fabrication Works provides rolling shutters, gates, aluminium work and custom metal fabrication in Louis Wadi, Thane West. We work with homeowners, shops, housing societies and commercial premises to discuss practical solutions for their spaces.

![Sahu Fabrication Works — shutters, gates, aluminium windows and our red-and-white awning](docs/motion/sahu-built-in-motion.gif)

## What we do

- **Rolling shutters:** manual, gear-operated and motorised options, including steel, aluminium, polycarbonate, perforated and powder-coated shutters.
- **Gates & safety doors:** fabricated gates, MS gates, safety doors and manual or motorised sliding gates.
- **Aluminium work:** sliding windows, aluminium fabrication and window installation.
- **Sheds & awnings:** car parking sheds and outdoor covers planned around the available space.
- **Custom fabrication:** general metalwork and construction-related fabrication based on your requirements.
- **PVC fittings:** divider and corner profiles for joining and finishing work.

## Made around your requirements

Every opening and every site is different. Bring your measurements, a drawing or an idea, and we can discuss the layout, materials, finish and operating options that suit your needs. Final specifications and installation requirements are agreed for each project.
