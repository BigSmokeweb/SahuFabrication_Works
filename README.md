## About Sahu Fabrication Works

Sahu Fabrication Works provides fabrication solutions for residential, commercial and construction requirements. Based in Louis Wadi, Thane West, our workshop offers rolling shutters, gates, safety doors, aluminium windows, sheds, awnings and general metalwork.

We discuss each project in relation to its dimensions, intended use and site conditions. From manual and motorised operation to material and finish options, the aim is to agree a practical solution suited to your space.

## Products and services

- **Rolling shutters:** manual, gear-operated and motorised options, including steel, aluminium, polycarbonate, perforated and powder-coated shutters.
- **Gates & safety doors:** fabricated gates, MS gates, safety doors and manual or motorised sliding gates.
- **Aluminium work:** sliding windows, aluminium fabrication and window installation.
- **Sheds & awnings:** car parking sheds and outdoor covers planned around the available space.
- **Custom fabrication:** general metalwork and construction-related fabrication based on your requirements.
- **PVC fittings:** divider and corner profiles for joining and finishing work.

## Our approach

We begin with your requirements. Bring your measurements, a drawing or a reference image so we can discuss the layout, materials, finish and operating options. Fabrication details and installation scope are confirmed for the individual project.

Our services cover homes, housing societies, retail shops, garages, offices and other commercial premises. For site-specific or construction-related work, visit the workshop to discuss your requirements in detail.
