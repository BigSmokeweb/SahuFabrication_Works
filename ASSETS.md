# Asset provenance

## Company motion edit

`docs/motion/index.html` is the self-contained, silent 25-second “Built in motion” company promo. The owner approved the assembling-metal and product-reveal direction. One continuous camera rig follows a slatted shutter assembly, shutter lift, gate movement, sliding-window treatment and the original company awning. The red, white, orange and warm neutrals come from the existing brand palette. No new business claims or real-project imagery were added.

`docs/motion/sahu-built-in-motion.gif` is the README preview, rendered at 720 × 406 and 10 fps from that timeline. `scripts/render-company-motion.mjs` reproduces it using the installed Playwright Chromium and FFmpeg. No changes to the website's runtime dependencies were needed.

The HTML embeds the existing awning SVG and three existing illustrative product images as data URLs. Its display typeface is Barlow Condensed SemiBold from Google Fonts, distributed under the SIL Open Font License included at `docs/motion/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. The inline GSAP 3.12.5 runtime retains its license header. Bang Motion attribution and its MIT license are included at `docs/motion/BANG-MOTION-LICENSE.txt`.

- `public/images/hero.webp`: original AI-generated illustrative product concept, built-in ImageGen. It does not represent a completed Sahu project or photograph of the business. Generation source was saved in the Codex generated-images directory and converted to optimized WebP in this repository.
- Prompt: “Photorealistic architectural product visualization of a charcoal steel rolling shutter in a clean industrial workshop facade, adjacent subtle sliding gate, warm sunlight, concrete floor, navy and grey tones, wide composition with darker left space for website text, no people, logos, text or watermark; illustrative concept, not a claimed real project.”
- `public/images/{shutter,gate,window,shed,fabrication,pvc}.svg`: original code-authored product illustrations generated by `scripts/create-assets.mjs`. Not real project photos. Regenerate SVGs with `node scripts/create-assets.mjs`; optionally pass a source image path to regenerate hero WebP.
- Lucide icons: lucide-react, ISC licensed, installed via npm. System fonts only; no third-party font request.
- `public/favicon.ico`: the company awning rendered by `scripts/create-favicon.mjs`.
- The retained product SVG illustrations use the earlier nine-color grayscale palette. The existing hero photograph is displayed with CSS grayscale on the homepage and About page; its source bitmap is preserved.
- No logos, customer photos, certifications or text copied from the reference website. Layout and visual progression were used as design references.

## Photorealistic product visuals

Six original images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, visually reviewed, and saved locally as optimized 1536 × 1024 WebP assets:

- `public/images/products/rolling-shutters.webp`: a steel shopfront shutter.
- `public/images/products/gates-and-doors.webp`: a fabricated steel sliding entrance gate.
- `public/images/products/aluminium-work.webp`: an aluminium sliding window.
- `public/images/products/sheds-and-awnings.webp`: a steel car-parking canopy.
- `public/images/products/general-fabrication.webp`: a welded steel frame and metal sections.
- `public/images/products/pvc-fittings.webp`: PVC corner and divider profiles.

The exact generation prompts and output mappings are recorded in [docs/product-image-prompts.json](docs/product-image-prompts.json). All images use monochrome lighting and materials retained alongside the current red, orange and white brand theme. Optimized files range from approximately 99–220 KB each.

These are illustrative AI-generated product visualizations, not photographs of Sahu's premises or completed projects. They replace the original SVG drawings in the active category cards, service listings and details, gallery, related hero slides and homepage gallery preview. A service variant may show a visual of its broader category; the detail-page notice identifies it as a range illustration rather than a verified exact specification. Original SVG sources remain in the repository.

Verification for the product-image update: lint, strict TypeScript and the production build passed. All 21 browser checks passed across Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, including route/image loading, gallery interactions, responsive layouts and the tested accessibility checks. The images and their gallery presentation were also visually reviewed. No real-project photography or verified product specifications are implied by these illustrative assets.

## Company logo

The owner supplied a photograph of the company business card on November 21, 2026. Its red-and-white striped fabric awning was reconstructed as native SVG artwork, with the company name underneath in warm orange italic serif lettering. This is a vector reconstruction, not a crop of the photographed card or an AI-generated logo.

- `public/images/sahu-awning.svg`: standalone fabric awning used in the header, footer and favicon generation.
- `public/images/sahu-logo.svg`: transparent complete logo with the company name below the fabric.
- `public/images/sahu-logo.png`: transparent 1080 × 360 raster export of the complete logo.
- The website uses accessible HTML text below the awning so the company name stays readable at responsive sizes. The footer uses lighter orange for contrast.

The photograph itself is not bundled or published. Its contact details were not imported as part of this logo and theme update. The digital brand colors and verification are documented in `PALETTE.md`.
